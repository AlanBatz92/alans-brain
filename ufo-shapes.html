The UAP Shape Census

ℹ️ How this census is built

Each source (book, report, interview transcript) is ingested locally and normalized into located text segments. A controlled shape vocabulary — disc, sphere, triangle, cigar, Tic-Tac, egg, cone, diamond, cube, and more — is matched against every segment, and each hit is logged with its source, its location in the text, and the surrounding snippet so the mention can be checked against the original.

This is a high-recall lexical pass. It deliberately catches everything and will include false positives (a "disc" that means a record, a "triangle" that means a love triangle). A planned disambiguation pass will confirm each mention actually describes a craft and extract its descriptive modifiers. Counts shown here are mentions, not confirmed distinct sightings — a single famous case described across ten books counts ten times. Read the snippets, not just the numbers.
