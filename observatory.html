ℹ️ How confidence & the life list work

Every 15-second clip is run through BirdNET, which scores how sure it is (0–100%) for each species it thinks it hears. That score is the model's certainty — not a calibrated probability — and distance from the mic, overlapping calls, wind, and traffic all drag it down.

What the page shows

  • The species grid and analytics show detections at 85%+ (the display floor), for clean local data.
  • The box quietly keeps everything down to 60%, so a bird card's "recent hits" can show the lower, less-certain detections — which is exactly what explains why a frequently-heard bird isn't a lifer yet.

Making the life list (any one of three)

  • 3 detections at 85%+ within a rolling 24 hours — a sustained, confident presence in one day.
  • One ~100% detection — an unmistakable single hit.
  • 8 detections at 70%+, all-time, with no time limit — the cumulative-evidence path. A bird heard often but never quite at 85% (a backyard regular like the Downy Woodpecker) is still very unlikely to be repeated noise, so the weight of evidence earns it a spot.
