Witness Scenes

See what a witness described, placed where it would have had to be.
← Back to Paranormal

Each scene is rebuilt from a witness's own words: where they stood, which way they looked, how big the object was against a thumb at arm's length, how long they watched. You see it from their eyes, to scale, and can swap in the ordinary explanations people suggest (a balloon, a cloud, a drone, a helicopter) to judge for yourself whether any of them could have looked like that. It is a way to see testimony, not a verdict on it. Every setting links back to the exact words it came from.

How to read a scene

  • Witness eye is the view from where they stood; Zoom 3x helps on a phone.
  • Every candidate sits in the same spot at the same size, because that is what was reported. What changes is how far away it would have to be, and that decides whether it fits everything else they said.
  • Play the event runs the sighting, the glance away and the look back, with each candidate drifting in that day's measured winds.
  • Claim tags like C18 open the exact quote behind a setting.
